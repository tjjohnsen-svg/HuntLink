HuntLinq privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026

HuntLinq is a free, simple map that lets a hunting group see each other. It is built to collect as little as possible. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold.

What HuntLinq stores

  • The name you type. Use any name you want. It is what your group sees on the map.
  • Your location, only while you share. Sharing is off until you tap Start sharing. While it is on, HuntLinq stores your latest spot, its accuracy and when it was measured. It keeps only the latest spot, not a trail.
  • A random ID for this phone. HuntLinq signs in anonymously, so there is no email, phone number, password account or birthday.
  • Which group you are in. Plus your role if you started or help run a group.

Who can see it

Only the people in your group can see your name and your shared location. The Hunters list on the group map explains who can see you. Nobody outside your group, including other HuntLinq groups, can see it.

How long it is kept

  • When you stop sharing, your shared spot is deleted.
  • If you lose signal while sharing, your group keeps seeing your last spot for up to 6 hours, then it is removed.
  • When you leave a group, or the group leader removes you, your name and spot are deleted from that group.
  • On your phone, HuntLinq keeps your name, your settings, offline maps, waypoints and a copy of your group's last known spots for the offline map. Leaving the group clears the group copy. Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data removes the rest.

Where it is stored

Group data is stored in Google Firebase, a cloud database run by Google, under Google's own security and privacy terms. Maps come from the free US Geological Survey National Map, and public land boundaries come from state and federal public map services. Those map services see ordinary map requests from your phone, but not your group or your name.

Safety

  • Only share with people you know and trust. Anyone in your group can see you while you share.
  • You can stop sharing or leave a group at any time.
  • HuntLinq needs cell signal to share and is not an emergency service. Carry a satellite messenger or other safety device in the backcountry.

Kids

HuntLinq is not meant for children under 13 on their own. A young hunter should use it with a parent or guardian who is in the same group.

Questions or deletion requests

Ask your group leader, or contact the HuntLinq owner through HuntLinq's project page. Never post a location or a group link there.
